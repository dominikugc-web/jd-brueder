## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus minimal vanilla JS), hosted on GitHub Pages in `dominikugc-web/jd-brueder`. Confirmed by the user in the ergebnis-zuerst plan. Fonts self-hosted, no tracking, no third-party embeds.

## Users

Private car owners in the Westerwald (Puderbach and surroundings) with Pkw/SUV/Van, owners of premium or enthusiast cars, lessees before returning a car, people preparing a car for sale. For dry-ice blasting also businesses (workshops, oldtimer owners, agriculture, industry, homeowners). Most visitors arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or Google Maps, and want to know: what do they do, what does it cost, how do I reach them.

## Product Purpose

The website of JD Brüder GbR, a car detailing business run by the brothers Jonathan and David Apelt (founded 2022). Success = a visitor understands offer and prices within seconds and contacts the business via WhatsApp or phone.

## Positioning

A two-brother family workshop in the Westerwald with a professional detailing studio (hexagon-light ceiling), dry-ice blasting (Dry Ice Energy), film/wrap and interior upgrades — handcraft pride and premium care without a corporate feel.

## Product Principles

1. Contact in one tap: a round WhatsApp button sits bottom right, call is in the header.
2. Prices in the open, honest "ab" wording.
3. Show the work, not adjectives — own photos and before/after over long texts.
4. Family workshop, premium standard: proud but grounded.
5. Legally clean: Impressum, Datenschutz, no tracking without consent.
